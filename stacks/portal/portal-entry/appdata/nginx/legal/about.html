Faviann

Faviann is a private, non-commercial home server run by one person for family and friends. It hosts personal media and everyday tools: movies and TV, music, books and audiobooks, photos, and recipes.

Everyone signs in once at auth.faviann.com, with a password, a security key, or Discord or Google. New accounts stay inactive until the administrator approves them, and each person reaches only the applications they were given.

Signing in with Discord or Google only identifies you: the service receives your name, email address, and account identifier, and never your password for those services.
