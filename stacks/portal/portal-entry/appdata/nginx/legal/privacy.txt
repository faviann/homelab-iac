Privacy Policy - faviann.com
Last updated: 2026-11-14

faviann.com is a private, non-commercial home server run by one person (the
"administrator") for family and friends. This policy covers the sign-in service
at auth.faviann.com and the applications you reach through it.

WHAT WE COLLECT

When you create an account, directly or with Discord or Google:
- your username, display name, and email address;
- a password you choose, stored only as a one-way hash;
- if you register a security key: its public key, never anything secret;
- if you sign in with Discord or Google: the account identifier,
  name, and email address that provider shares with us. We never receive
  your password for those services.

When you use the service:
- sign-in events with their date, IP address, and browser details, kept in
  the sign-in service's logs;
- whatever you choose to store in the applications themselves, such as
  watch history, reading progress, or uploaded files.

HOW WE USE IT

- To sign you in and decide which applications you can reach.
- To let the administrator review and approve new accounts. A sign-up
  notification with your username, display name, email address, and IP
  address is sent to the administrator's private Discord channel.
- To send account emails, such as address verification, through an email
  delivery provider.
- To set up your account in applications that need their own login, such as
  the music server, which receives your username and password.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information for advertising or any other
purpose. We use no analytics or advertising trackers.

WHERE IT IS STORED

On hardware the administrator owns and operates. Only the administrator has
access.

YOUR CHOICES

- You can connect or disconnect Discord or Google sign-in from your
  account settings at any time.
- You can ask the administrator to show, correct, or delete your account and
  its data. Deleting your account removes your sign-in data. Data inside
  individual applications is deleted on request.

CONTACT

Contact the administrator, the person who approved your account, for any
question or request about your data.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
